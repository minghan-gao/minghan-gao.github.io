**Role:** Research Intern · **Collaborators:** Thijs Roumen and Tobias Weinberg, Cornell · **Period:** Exploratory research, 2026

## Research question

When does adapting a synthetic voice help an AAC user express themself, and when does the added interaction cost interrupt conversation?

## Approach

- Examined how AAC users might decide when and how to change expressive voice style during conversations with familiar partners.
- Designed low-cost alternatives to explicit tagging, including inferred suggestions, selectable options, and lightweight controls.
- Framed an evaluation around self-expression, partner interpretation, conversational flow, and control burden.

This page describes exploratory research rather than a completed study.
