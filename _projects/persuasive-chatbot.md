**Role:** Research Assistant · **Advisor:** Susan Fussell, Cornell · **Period:** September–December 2023

## Overview

I helped build a web-based research platform for controlled studies of human–AI conversation and persuasion.

## My contribution

- Designed and implemented the responsive frontend in React, HTML, and CSS.
- Integrated GPT-4 through the OpenAI API for real-time chatbot interaction.
- Added automatic conversation logging to support researcher review and analysis.

This project introduced me to the practical details of building reliable interfaces for human-subjects research.
