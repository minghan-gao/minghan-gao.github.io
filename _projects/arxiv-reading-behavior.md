**Role:** Research Intern · **Advisor:** Yian Yin, Cornell · **Period:** June–August 2024

## Overview

I analyzed activity from 4.8 million arXiv users to investigate how researchers discover and engage with scholarly work.

## My contribution

- Filtered automated traffic to focus on consistent human readers.
- Built structured user timelines combining paper access dates, referral sources, and engagement depth.
- Analyzed referral-driven patterns in reading recency, team composition, and paper visibility.

The project strengthened my interest in computational social science methods for understanding real-world information behavior.
