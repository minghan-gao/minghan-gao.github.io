**Role:** Visiting Researcher · **Advisor:** Justin Chan, Carnegie Mellon University · **Period:** September 2026–present

## Research question

How can vision-language models interpret head-mounted procedural video well enough to assess performance and provide useful corrective feedback—not merely identify step boundaries?

## What I am working on

- Developing a framework for egocentric video understanding under motion blur, operator variability, and changing tool distance.
- Investigating zero- and few-shot recognition of procedural steps, active tools, and arbitrary execution errors.
- Designing evidence-grounded reasoning that connects an observed error to a likely cause and an actionable correction.

This work is ongoing; results will be added after evaluation.
