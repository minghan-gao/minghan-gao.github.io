**Role:** Research Assistant · **Collaborators:** Cheng Zhang and Hyunchul Lim, Cornell · **Period:** February–September 2024

## Overview

Sign-to-speech systems typically communicate linguistic content while losing emotional expression. This work examined how synthesized emotional voice can carry more of a signer's intended affect in Deaf-to-hearing communication.

## My contribution

- Engineered a neural text-to-speech pipeline spanning five emotional categories.
- Designed and deployed randomized Qualtrics studies with 93 hearing and 48 Deaf or hard-of-hearing participants.
- Processed approximately 100 ASL videos, aligning synthesized voice with the signed content and controlling presentation order.
- Led statistical analysis and visualization using Kruskal–Wallis tests, ANOVA, post-hoc comparisons, and confusion matrices.
