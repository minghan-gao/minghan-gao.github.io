**Role:** UX research and interaction design · **Period:** August–December 2025

## Challenge

How might a community ride-sharing service support low-income and disabled riders while addressing safety, accessibility, and trust?

## Process

- Conducted research with riders, TCAT staff, and local advocates to identify unmet mobility needs.
- Synthesized findings into design requirements for ride matching, medical trips, and accessibility-support workflows.
- Developed interactive Figma prototypes and refined them through expert reviews and user testing.
