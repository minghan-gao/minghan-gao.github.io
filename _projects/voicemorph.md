**Role:** Research Intern · **Collaborators:** Pattie Maes and Kye Shimizu, MIT Media Lab · **Period:** May–September 2025

## Overview

VoiceMorph is a speech-driven interface for real-time cross-individual voice transformation. We used it to study when a transformed voice stops feeling like one's own.

## My contribution

- Built a real-time experimental system in TouchDesigner around a deep-learning voice-morphing model.
- Automated accuracy and reaction-time capture across more than 2,000 trials with 21 participants.
- Performed quantitative analysis of perceptual thresholds and demographic, cognitive, and acoustic factors.
- Led acoustic quality control using UTMOSv2 and demographic matching, then synthesized quantitative and qualitative findings.
